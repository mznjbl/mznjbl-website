This notice describes our current contact setup. We will update it if hosting, analytics, or contact processing changes.

Information you submit

When you submit an inquiry, we collect your name, email address, project type, and message. These details are stored in our Cloudflare D1 database and used to respond to your inquiry. We also send an email notification to our business inbox using Google Apps Script.

External services

Our website is hosted on Cloudflare Workers, and contact inquiries are stored using Cloudflare D1. Google Apps Script processes inquiry details to send notifications to our Gmail inbox. These services may process information according to their respective privacy policies. Cloudflare may also process technical connection logs for security and operational purposes.

Analytics and cookies

We do not currently use visitor-tracking scripts or intentionally set analytics cookies on this website. Browser and hosting-provider operational logs may still exist under the hosting provider's policies.

Contact and requests
