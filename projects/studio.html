THE BRIEF

START WITH THE STUDIO.

It started as a simple idea: build myself a digital portfolio.

Simple, apparently, was asking too much.

I spent months thinking about the design, changing my mind, and figuring out how I wanted everything to look. At some point, a personal portfolio turned into an entire digital studio.

The acid green? I'm a Lando Norris fan. I liked the colour, so naturally, it became part of the whole identity. Very scientific decision-making.

As for the system messages, technical labels, and slightly robotic personality? I just like that kind of stuff. There's something satisfying about making a website feel like a system with its own character.

Originally, the name was supposed to be JUBL/. Short, simple, and taken from my father's name. I thought I'd figured out the branding.

Then I discovered that apparently, I wasn't the only one who liked that name.

Cue the identity crisis. Followed by another identity crisis. And probably a few more than necessary.

Eventually, JUBL/ evolved into MZNJBL/, combining Mizan and Jubli. Same personal meaning, just with a few extra letters and significantly more emotional damage.

The slash stayed, though. Apparently, even my name needed a software update.

And that's how MZNJBL/ happened. A portfolio that got a little out of hand.
